### Guitar Hero On Tour: Decades
This game does not have a traditional campaign in which songs become progressively more difficult.  Each time period consists of four Setlist Songs followed by an unlockable Encore Song, while three Victory Songs are unlocked after completing every song in the game.  Setlist, Encore, and Victory Songs are valued at 2, 3, and 4, respectively.  Intensity is the average value across the regions in which the song appears, rounded down.
